# Code of Conduct

Be kind, constructive, and respectful. People with different backgrounds and
levels of experience should feel comfortable asking questions, sharing ideas,
and contributing.

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, sexualized conduct,
trolling, and publishing someone else's private information are not acceptable.
Disagree with ideas without attacking people.

This applies in repository discussions and anywhere someone is representing the
Viritura community. Maintainers may edit or remove content, limit participation,
or ban people when needed to keep the community safe and productive.

Report conduct concerns privately to
[support@viritura.com](mailto:support@viritura.com), not in a public issue.
Maintainers will review reports as promptly and privately as practical.
